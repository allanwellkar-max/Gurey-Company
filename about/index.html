Our Story

Building Technology for Somalia's Future

Gurey Company was founded in 2020 in Mogadishu with a clear mission: to bridge the technology gap in Somalia and the Horn of Africa. We recognized that Somali businesses deserved access to world-class software solutions, and we set out to make that a reality. From humble beginnings, we have grown into a full-service technology company trusted by organizations across the region.

Today, we partner with businesses, government agencies, and non-profits to deliver custom software, modern websites, powerful mobile applications, and data-driven solutions. Our team combines deep local expertise with international best practices to create technology that works for the Somali market and beyond.
